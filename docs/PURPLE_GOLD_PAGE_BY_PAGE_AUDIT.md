## Motion assignment

- Buttons: fast purple→gold orbit, faster on hover, press-safe on touch.
- Cards: slow orbital border with subtle bloom; metric cards get a measured pulse.
- Auth: slow dual-aura border around identity surfaces.
- Map/Journey: route/safety panels get a directional signal animation.
- Admin/Data: slower, lower-opacity border circulation.
- Inputs: purple/gold focus-only animation.
- Danger/SOS: red semantic styling remains; animated border is supplementary only.
- Offscreen elements: border orbit is paused by IntersectionObserver for performance.
- Dynamic DOM: MutationObserver decorates cards/buttons inserted after initial load.
- Reduced motion: animated orbit stops and becomes a static premium gradient border.
